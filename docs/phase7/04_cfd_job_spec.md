# 04 - Canonical CFDJobSpec & ASME V&V 20 QC Engine

AASI enforces rigorous standards for CFD simulations generated by the autonomous research loop.

## 1. Canonical CFDJobSpec Schema

The `CFDJobSpec` is a self-contained, machine-readable JSON object that specifies every parameter required to execute a high-fidelity CFD simulation:

## 2. Automated Quality Control Engine (`CFDQualityControlEngine`)

Ingested CFD results undergo automated evaluation before inclusion into the dataset factory:

### 2.1 Residual Convergence Check
All governing equation residuals (continuity, momentum, turbulent kinetic energy $k$, specific dissipation $\omega$) must satisfy:
$$\max_{i \in \text{equations}} r_i \le 1.0 \times 10^{-4}$$

### 2.2 Force Stability Audit
Aerodynamic forces ($C_L, C_D$) must be monitored over the final $10\%$ of solver iterations. The relative variation $\Delta_{\text{force}}$ must satisfy:
$$\frac{\max(C_L) - \min(C_L)}{\text{mean}(C_L)} \le 0.01 \quad (1.0\%)$$

### 2.3 Physical Positivity Check
In accordance with classical fluid mechanics, total drag must be strictly positive in viscous flow:
$$C_D > 0.0001$$
Any simulation returning zero or negative drag is immediately rejected as non-physical.

### 2.4 Pressure Distribution Envelope
Surface pressure coefficients $C_p(x/c)$ are checked against the stagnation condition:
$$C_p \le 1.0 + \frac{1}{4} M^2 + \dots$$
Points exceeding the physical total pressure limit are flagged as divergence artifacts.
