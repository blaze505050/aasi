## 2. Outreach Cadence & Tracking Protocol

### Weekly Cadence
* **Monday:** Research and verify direct LinkedIn & work emails for 5 accounts.
* **Tuesday:** Send personalized value-first cold messages (Script 1) offering 1 free verified aerodynamic database.
* **Wednesday–Thursday:** Generate requested airfoil/wing database within 48 hours of response.
* **Friday:** Deliver database package and schedule 15-minute feedback and conversion call.

### Conversion Pipeline Stages
1. **Identified:** Decision-maker verified on LinkedIn.
2. **Contacted:** Initial value-first message sent.
3. **Engaged:** Prospect replied with airfoil profile or platform specs.
4. **Delivered (Free Pilot):** Full polar deck, DATCOM namelist, and ASME V&V 20 PDF delivered.
5. **Review Call Completed:** Feedback recorded, objections handled.
6. **Proposal Submitted:** Service package (₹75,000–₹1,50,000) or SaaS Team license sent.
7. **Closed / Won:** Contract executed, advance payment received.
